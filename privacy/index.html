Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026
The short version: Solitaire doesn’t collect, store or share any information about you. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no accounts of our own.

Information we collect

None. The app has no servers of its own and sends nothing about you or how you play to us or to anyone else. It contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software.

What stays on your device

Your games in progress, statistics, Daily Deal results and settings are saved only on your iPhone or iPad. They are never uploaded. Deleting the app deletes them.

Apple services

  • Game Center (optional). If you’re signed in to Game Center, Apple provides your player name and photo to the app so it can show them. Game Center is operated by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • In-app purchases. Purchases and Restore Purchases are handled entirely by the App Store. We never see your payment details or Apple Account.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
