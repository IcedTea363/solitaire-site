Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026
The short version: Solitaire doesn’t collect, store or share any information about you. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no accounts of our own. The app works fully offline.

This policy covers the Solitaire app for iPhone and iPad, made by Eric Nail, an individual developer.

Information we collect

None. The app has no servers of its own and sends nothing about you or how you play to us or to anyone else. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software, and it doesn’t track you across other apps or websites. Its App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected.

What stays on your device

Your games in progress, statistics, trophies, Daily Deal results, settings and a record of what you’ve bought are saved only on your iPhone or iPad, in the app’s own storage. They are never uploaded and aren’t synced to iCloud. You can reset a game’s statistics in the app (Main Menu › Stats › Reset Statistics). Deleting the app deletes all of this data.

Apple services

  • Game Center (optional). If you choose to sign in to Game Center, Apple provides your player name and photo to the app so it can show them, and the app reports the trophies you earn to Game Center as achievements. Game Center is Apple’s service, operated under Apple’s Privacy Policy; we don’t receive that information. You can sign out in iOS Settings › Game Center, and the app works the same without it.
  • In-app purchases. Purchases and Restore Purchases are handled by the App Store (StoreKit) and verified on your device. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details or your Apple Account.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
