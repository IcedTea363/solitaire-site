Support

Help for Solitaire on iPhone and iPad · Last updated: October 31, 2026

How do I play?

Tap the ☰ button to open the Main Menu, then tap Rules for how each game works. Drag a card or a run where it should go, or just tap a card and it goes to its best spot. Hint shows a good next move and Undo takes back as many moves as you like.

What’s free, and what does Full Unlock add?

Klondike (Draw 1 and Draw 3) is free, with no ads and no time limits. Full Unlock is a one-time purchase that adds Spider, FreeCell, Pyramid and extra themes. There are no subscriptions. Accessibility features are always free.

I bought Full Unlock — how do I get it on another device?

Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple Account, tap ☰ to open the Main Menu, tap Store, then Restore Purchases. Full Unlock also works with Family Sharing.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are made through the App Store, so refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Do I need the internet or an account?

No. Everything, including the Daily Deal, works offline, and there’s no account to create. Signing in to Game Center is optional; it shows your name and photo and lets you see your achievements. To sign out, use iOS Settings › Game Center.

Does the Daily Deal need the internet?

No. Each day’s deal is worked out on your device and is the same for everyone. Every Daily Deal can be won.

Can every game be won?

Turn on Settings › Winnable Deals Only (tap ⚙) and new games are ones the built-in solver has proven can be won. (Four-suit Spider is too deep to prove, so it isn’t covered.)

How do I reset my statistics?

Open the Main Menu, tap Stats, pick the game, then tap Reset Statistics for that game and confirm. Games played, wins, streaks and bests for that game go back to zero.

Watching something while you play?

Tap the Picture in Picture button at the top of the table to make room for the video; touch and hold it to choose a corner or the full width. Tap it again to give the room back.

Accessibility

Tap ⚙ for Settings. The Accessibility section has Four-Color Deck, Large Card Index and Left-Handed Layout. The table works with VoiceOver: each pile is read aloud card by card, double-tap plays its best move, and every legal move is available as an action. With Reduce Motion on, the moving table effects are turned off. Accessibility features are always free.

Will my progress sync between devices?

No — to keep everything private, games and statistics stay on the device you play on. Deleting the app deletes them. Your purchases are tied to your Apple Account and can always be restored.
