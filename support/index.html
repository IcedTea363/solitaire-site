Help for Solitaire on iPhone and iPad

How do I play?

Open the ☰ menu and tap Rules for each game. Drag a card or a run where it should go, or just tap a card and it goes to its best spot. Hint shows a good next move and Undo takes back as many moves as you like.

I bought Full Unlock — how do I get it on another device?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open the ☰ menu, tap Store, then Restore Purchases. Full Unlock also works with Family Sharing.

Does the Daily Deal need the internet?

No. Each day’s deal is worked out on your device and is the same for everyone. Every Daily Deal can be won.

Can every game be won?

Turn on Settings › Winnable Deals Only and new games are ones the built-in solver has proven can be won.

Watching something while you play?

Tap the Picture in Picture button at the top of the table to move the table down and leave room for the video.

Accessibility

Settings › Accessibility has a four-color deck, a large card index and a left-handed layout. The table works with VoiceOver, and animations follow Reduce Motion. Accessibility features are always free.

Will my progress sync between devices?

No — to keep everything private, games and statistics stay on the device you play on.
